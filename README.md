# KillMyParents
Easily remove Xbox 360 Parental/Content Controls, even without knowing the password. KillMyParents is a compilation of Xbox 360 homebrew software set up to make the process as easy as putting the files on a USB drive, plugging it into the Xbox 360, and clicking a few buttons. That's it!

This software is intended to simplify and streamline Xbox 360 parental/content control removal to help with cases such as selling/giving/buying/receiving an Xbox 360 that has a parental control lock password that is unknown and needs to be wiped.

## How It Works
KillMyParents uses Derf's Parental Control Remover, an Xbox 360 homebrew application, to remove Parental/Content Controls from the Xbox 360 by simply pressing a button; no need to know the password. However, because Parental Control Remover is a homebrew application (e.g. unsigned code), it will NOT run on an unmodified console, meaning the Xbox 360 must be running some sort of exploit for this to work. That's where BadUpdate and ABadAvatar come in, allowing the console to be temporarily hacked (only until it is turned off) so the Parental Control Remover software can run. BadUpdate and ABadAvatar require the latest Xbox 360 Dashboard, which is bundled with KillMyParents. When you insert the KillMyParents USB stick into your Xbox 360, a pop-up will automatically show, prompting you to update, if your Xbox 360 is not on the latest dashboard. That's why this software exists, to eliminate extra steps and navigate the nuances to make it as easy, quick, and straightforward as possible to remove parental controls, especially if you don't want your Xbox 360 permanently modified (KillMyParents leaves no trace and the console returns to its previous unmodified state when the system is shut down and the USB is removed).

## Features
- Quick and easy, out-of-the-box, all you need is a USB drive
- Works on ANY unmodified Xbox 360 (modified Xbox 360s should just use Derf's Parental Control Remover directly)
- Comes with an update to the latest version of the Xbox 360 Dashboard, and automates the update process if needed
- Does NOT permanently hack the console, leaves it unmodified afterwards

# Instructions

## IMPORTANT: DISCONNECT FROM THE INTERNET/XBOX LIVE before continuing, or you may risk your console being permanently banned.

### Step 1: Sign out of ALL profiles
- Failing to do so will likely cause issues such as freezing when trying to run KillMyParents. Shouldn't cause permanent damage, however.

### Step 2: Format the USB Stick
- Insert the USB stick of your choice (MAKE SURE THERE IS NO IMPORTANT DATA ON IT - IT WILL BE WIPED) into a USB port on your Xbox 360
- On your Xbox 360, navigate to Settings > System > Storage
- Highlight your USB stick and Press Y, then press Format. Wait for the process to complete.

### Step 3: Put KillMyParents onto the USB Stick
- Once the format has completed, remove the USB stick from your Xbox 360 and insert it into your computer
- Download the latest KillMyParents.zip file from the Releases and extract its contents to the root of your USB drive
  - If prompted, MERGE all folders and REPLACE all files

### Step 4: Run KillMyParents
- Once everything has been successfully copied over, safely eject the USB stick from your computer and insert it into your Xbox 360 (make sure all profiles are signed out)
- If prompted, install the system update (this is NOT a hack, but an actual system update. It is safe to install and will not modify your console.)
- On the Xbox 360 dashboard, navigate to the "social" tab and click "Sign In or Out"
- The ABadAvatar exploit should launch automatically. You should see the chain breaking video. If you do not see this video after 10 seconds, simply turn off the Xbox 360 and restart Step 4 (MAKE SURE TO SIGN OUT IF YOUR XBOX 360 AUTOMATICALLY SIGNS IN TO A CERTAIN PROFILE)
- When you reach the XeUnshackle Max screen, press the Back button (NOT "B", the Back button)
- You should see a screen that says "Parental Control Remover". Simply press A to clear Parental Controls.
- That's it! You can now turn off your console and remove the USB stick. Your console will return to its unmodified state when you power it on.
  - NOTE: If you press B to reboot and leave the USB stick plugged in, Step 4 may loop itself unnecessarily. Power down the console and remove the USB stick to finish.
